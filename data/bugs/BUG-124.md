# BUG-124: Call Recording Audio Permanently Muted When Agent Toggles Hold State

## Summary
When an agent puts a caller on hold and subsequently resumes the call, the customer channel in the final stored MP3 recording is muted for the remainder of the conversation.

## Reported Environment
Production

## Root Cause
The audio mixer pipeline paused the inbound audio sink on the `HOLD_START` event, but the `HOLD_STOP` event listener failed to re-attach the G.711 audio decoder to the mixer track.

## Resolution
Refactored the WebRTC media pipeline to continuously mix silence comfort noise rather than detaching audio tracks during hold states.
